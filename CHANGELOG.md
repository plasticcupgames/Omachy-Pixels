# Omachy Pixels 1.0.0

Initial release by Tim Hyatt / Tim Hyatt Games.

- Original Pixels In Space ships, enemies, bosses, projectiles, and effects.
- Friendly and enemy squadron formations with leader replacement.
- Animated engine exhaust with continuous, drifting, fading trails.
- Desktop-only battle that pauses behind active applications.
- Bar controls for monitor, fleet size, activity, area, and pause.
- Offline operation and persistent per-user preferences.
- MIT code, OFL font, and separately licensed original artwork.
